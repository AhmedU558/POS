topic: Integrated POS, Inventory & Business Management System
updated: 2026-08-25T13:07
---

- (decision) Distill PRD and SRS into unified SPEC.md
- (capability) CAP-1: Cashiers can process physical sales through open register sessions, handle payments, and issue receipts to complete transactions.
- (capability) CAP-2: Managers can receive, adjust, transfer, and track stock across locations and batches.
- (capability) CAP-3: Staff can manage customer profiles (with store credit) and supplier profiles (with purchase orders and payables).
- (capability) CAP-4: Cashiers can open, close, and reconcile register sessions with Z-reports.
- (capability) CAP-5: Staff can apply eligibility-based discounts/promotions and create convertable quotations for customers.
- (capability) CAP-6: Administrators can generate financial/operational reports and view immutable audit logs of privileged actions.
- (capability) CAP-7: The system can ingest and fulfill online orders using the unified inventory.
- (constraint) Financial and inventory operations must use database transactions for atomicity.
- (constraint) Authorization must be enforced on the server/API layer using least-privilege RBAC.
- (constraint) Closed register sessions and audit records must be protected from unauthorized modification.
- (constraint) All financial totals must use a consistent currency and configured precision.
- (constraint) Must support multiple POS terminals operating concurrently per store.
- (decision) Non-goals for MVP include Payroll, Employee Attendance, AI Forecasting, Customer Mobile App.
