SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION
Integrated POS, Inventory & Business Management System
SRS • Version 1.0 • Baseline


1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This SRS defines the functional, non-functional, security, data, workflow, validation, and acceptance requirements for the Integrated POS, Inventory & Business Management System. It is intended to provide a common implementation baseline for product owners, developers, designers, testers, database engineers, and deployment teams.
1.2 Scope
The system covers point-of-sale transactions, inventory and stock control, products, customers, suppliers, purchasing, accounts payable, cash registers, day-end closing, online orders, quotations, discounts and promotions, budgets, financial reporting, labels, receipts, authentication, authorization, notifications, and audit logging.
1.3 Intended Audience
Product owners and business stakeholders
Software architects and backend/frontend developers
Database engineers
UI/UX designers
QA and test engineers
System administrators and DevOps engineers
1.4 Definitions

2. Overall System Description
2.1 Product Perspective
The application will operate as a centralized business platform with a transactional POS interface, management dashboard, inventory services, financial modules, and optional online-order integration. All channels should use a common source of truth for products, customers, orders, inventory, and financial transactions.
POS Terminals ─┐
Online Orders ──┼──> Application/API Layer ──> Database
Admin Dashboard ┘             │
                              ├── Inventory
                              ├── Sales/POS
                              ├── Accounting/AP
                              └── Reporting
2.2 Deployment Scope
Single-store deployment must be supported.
Multiple terminals per store must be supported.
Architecture must allow future multi-store operation.
The application must support centralized administration and controlled access.
2.3 Assumptions
Products have unique internal identifiers.
Users are assigned roles and permissions.
Inventory-changing operations are recorded as transactions.
Financially significant operations are auditable.
Business tax and currency rules are configurable rather than hard-coded.
3. User Roles & Access Control

The authorization layer shall use least-privilege access. Sensitive operations must be controlled by explicit permissions rather than role names alone.

5. Business Rules

6. Validation Requirements
Required fields must be validated before persistence.
Numeric quantities must be greater than zero unless a specific adjustment workflow permits negative values.
Prices and monetary amounts must use validated numeric precision.
SKU/barcode uniqueness must be enforced according to configuration.
Dates must use valid calendar values.
Due dates and expiration dates must satisfy applicable business rules.
User permissions must be checked server-side for every protected operation.
Duplicate transaction submissions must be detected or made idempotent where applicable.
Inventory operations must prevent invalid negative stock where negative stock is disabled.

8. Non-Functional Requirements

9. Security Requirements
Use secure authentication and password hashing.
Use role/permission authorization on all protected endpoints.
Validate and sanitize all external input.
Use parameterized database queries/ORM protections.
Use HTTPS in production.
Apply rate limiting to authentication and sensitive endpoints.
Protect secrets using environment/secret-management mechanisms.
Record privileged and financial operations in audit logs.
Restrict administrative functions by permission.
Implement secure session/token expiration and revocation strategy.

10.2 Data Integrity
Primary and foreign-key relationships shall enforce referential integrity.
Unique identifiers shall be enforced where required.
Financial and inventory operations shall use database transactions where atomicity is required.
Deleted business records should generally use controlled deactivation/voiding rather than destructive deletion when historical reporting depends on them.
